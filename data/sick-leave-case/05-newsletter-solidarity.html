Sick leave in 2026: two changes for employers

Dear client,

From 1 January 2026, two rules change for employers in Belgium when an employee is unable to work due to illness. Below we summarise what this means for you.

1. New solidarity contribution

Employers with at least 50 employees will contribute 30% of the sickness benefit paid during the second and third month of an employee's incapacity for work. The contribution applies to employees under the age of 55.

The first invoices are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026. You do not need to take any action at this stage. Your payroll consultant will contact you once the first calculation is available.

2. Longer relapse period

If an employee falls ill again within 8 weeks after returning to work (previously 14 days), the new absence is considered a continuation of the previous one. No new period of guaranteed salary starts.
