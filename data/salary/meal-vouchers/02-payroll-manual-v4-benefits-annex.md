ANNEX B  EXTRA-LEGAL BENEFITS


B.3  Meal vouchers

Meal vouchers are exempt from social security contributions and taxes if
all of the following conditions are met:

  a) they are granted by a collective agreement or an individual written
     agreement;
  b) the number of vouchers equals the number of days actually worked;
  c) the voucher is issued in the name of the employee;
  d) the employer part does not exceed EUR 6.91 per voucher;
  e) the employee part is at least EUR 1.09 per voucher;
  f) the voucher is valid for 12 months.

Maximum face value of a meal voucher: EUR 8.00.

Payroll codes:
  4100   Meal vouchers - employer part
  4105   Meal vouchers - employee part (deduction)


B.4  Eco vouchers

Maximum EUR 250 per year for a full-time employee, pro rata for part-time.
