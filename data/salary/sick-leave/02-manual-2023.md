CHAPTER 7  SICKNESS AND GUARANTEED SALARY


7.1  Guaranteed salary for white-collar employees

In case of incapacity for work due to illness or an accident outside work,
the white-collar employee keeps the right to his normal salary during the
first 30 days of incapacity. This salary is paid by the employer. From the
31st day the employee receives a benefit from his health insurance fund.


7.2  Relapse

When the employee falls ill again within 14 days after the end of a period
of incapacity for which guaranteed salary was paid, the new incapacity is
considered a continuation of the first one. Guaranteed salary is only paid
for the days that remain of the 30-day period.

When the employee falls ill again more than 14 days after returning to
work, a new period of guaranteed salary of 30 days starts.

  Example: the employee returns to work on 1 March and falls ill again on
  20 March. More than 14 days have passed. A new period of guaranteed
  salary starts on 20 March.


7.3  Payroll codes

  1010   Guaranteed salary – first month
  1015   Relapse within 14 days (continuation)
  1020   Sickness – no salary (health insurance fund)

7.4  Medical certificate

The employee must send a medical certificate within 2 working days, unless
the work regulations or the collective agreement provide otherwise.


7.5  Control doctor

The employer may send a control doctor at his own expense. The employee
cannot refuse the examination.
