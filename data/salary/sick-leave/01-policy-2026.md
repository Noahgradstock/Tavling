1. Purpose

This policy describes how payroll consultants process incapacity for work due to illness for white-collar employees of Belgian clients, in line with the changes that apply from 1 January 2026.


2. Scope

This policy applies to all white-collar employees (bedienden) employed by clients in Belgium, regardless of joint committee. Blue-collar employees are covered by PS-BE-POL-0413. It does not apply to clients in other countries.


3. Guaranteed salary

3.1 During the first 30 calendar days of incapacity for work, the employer continues to pay the full salary (guaranteed salary).

3.2 From day 31 onwards, the employee receives a sickness benefit from the health insurance fund.


4. Relapse

4.1 From 1 January 2026, the relapse period is 8 weeks (previously 14 days).

4.2 If the employee falls ill again within 8 weeks after returning to work, the new incapacity is considered a continuation of the previous one. No new period of guaranteed salary starts. The remaining days of the original 30-day period, if any, are paid by the employer.

4.3 If the 30 days of guaranteed salary have already been used in full, the health insurance fund pays from the first day of the new incapacity.

4.4 If the new incapacity starts more than 8 weeks after the employee returned to work, a new period of guaranteed salary starts.

4.5 The employee may prove, by means of a medical certificate, that the new incapacity has a different cause. In that case a new period of guaranteed salary starts, regardless of the 8-week period.

Example
An employee is ill from 3 March to 6 April 2026 (35 days; 30 days of guaranteed salary paid). The employee returns to work on 7 April and falls ill again on 28 April (3 weeks later). As this is within 8 weeks, no new guaranteed salary is due. The health insurance fund pays from 28 April.


5. Employer solidarity contribution

5.1 Employers with 50 or more employees pay a solidarity contribution equal to 30% of the sickness benefit for the second and third month of incapacity.

5.2 The contribution only applies to employees younger than 55 at the start of the incapacity.

5.3 The first collection is expected in Q4 2026. Clients will be informed by their payroll consultant once the calculation is available. See also the client newsletter of January 2026.


6. Processing in payroll

6.1 Check the date of return to work and the start date of the new incapacity.

6.2 If the interval is less than 8 weeks, do not restart the guaranteed salary codes (1010). Use continuation code 1016.

6.3 Flag the file for the solidarity contribution if the client has 50 or more employees and the employee is younger than 55.
