Indexation guide 2026 (draft estimate)

Author: Pieter Jacobs
Created: 15/11/2025
Status: Draft
Last modified: 15/11/2025


Purpose

Give consultants an early estimate of the January 2026 indexations so they
can inform clients who want to prepare their 2026 budget.

These figures are estimates based on the Federal Planning Bureau forecast of
November 2025. They are not the official percentages. Always check the
official notice of the joint committee before processing the January payroll.


Estimated indexation on 1 January 2026

Joint committee                      Estimated %     Official notice expected
PC 200 (white-collar, auxiliary)     2.1%            mid-December 2025
PC 118 (food industry)               2.2%            mid-December 2025
PC 124 (construction)                n/a (1 Jan + 1 Jul system)
PC 330 (health care)                 n/a (indexed via federal index)


How to communicate to clients

- Always say "estimated" and give the source (Planning Bureau).
- Do not change payroll parameters before the official notice.
- Once the notice is published, this page will be updated.
