MINIMUM BENEFIT IN KIND FOR A COMPANY CAR – 2026

Reference: FIN/VAA/2026

From 1 January 2026 the minimum taxable benefit in kind for the private use of a company car is €1,650 per year.

The benefit is calculated with the CO2 formula; if the result is lower, the minimum applies.
