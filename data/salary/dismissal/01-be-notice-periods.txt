NOTICE PERIODS FOR DISMISSAL BY THE EMPLOYER

Reference: WASO/OPZEG/2014

Since 1 January 2014 one uniform system applies to white-collar and blue-collar employees.

For an employee with 5 years of service the employer must give a notice period of 18 weeks.

From the sixth year on, the notice period grows by three weeks per year of service.
