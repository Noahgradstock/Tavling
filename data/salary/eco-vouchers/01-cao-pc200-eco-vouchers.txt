Joint Committee 200 (PC 200)

COLLECTIVE AGREEMENT ON ECO VOUCHERS

Reference: PC200/ECO/2024

1. Scope

This agreement applies to all white-collar employees under Joint Committee 200.

2. Amount

A full-time employee with a complete reference period receives eco vouchers worth €250 per year. Part-time employees receive a pro rata amount.

3. Payment

Eco vouchers are granted in June each year.
