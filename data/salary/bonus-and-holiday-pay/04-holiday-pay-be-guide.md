Holiday pay for white-collar employees (Belgium)

Owner: Anna De Smet, Payroll BE
Reviewed by: Legal Payroll BE, 10/01/2026
Last modified: 10/01/2026


1. Simple holiday pay

During their holidays the white-collar employee keeps the normal monthly
salary. This is paid by the employer with the regular payroll.


2. Double holiday pay

The double holiday pay is 92% of the gross monthly salary of the month in
which the main holiday is taken. It is usually paid in May or June, and at
the latest when the main holiday starts.

It is calculated on the holiday entitlement earned in the previous calendar
year (the holiday year follows the "vakantiedienstjaar").

  Example: gross monthly salary EUR 3,500
           double holiday pay = 3,500 x 92% = EUR 3,220


3. Blue-collar employees

Blue-collar employees receive their holiday pay from the holiday fund
(RJV/ONVA or a sector fund), not from the employer. Do not use this guide
for blue-collar employees.


4. Other countries

This guide applies to Belgium only. The Netherlands uses a holiday allowance
(vakantiebijslag) of at least 8% of the annual salary. See the Payroll NL
space on Confluence.
